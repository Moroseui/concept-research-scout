Findings that matter beyond the table:

- **C5 is downgraded to INCREMENTAL.** Tallam et al. (Radiology 2022, DOI 10.1148/radiol.211914) already predicts diabetes from automated pancreas CT features in ~9,000 patients with intrapancreatic fat as the *top-ranked* predictor, and it already includes the liver-fat and visceral-fat context C5 planned as controls. A 2024 study (PMC11226987, read in full text) validates the exact pancreas-to-spleen HU ratio, and a 2025 Academic Radiology paper already uses TotalSegmentator for these biomarkers. The only unduplicated element is decoding Merlin's learned head — a narrow delta over a mature literature, consistent with the candidate's own self-reported novelty score of 2.
- **C3 carries a scientific risk flag, recorded in the audit:** retrieved physiology literature states the upright craniocaudal blood-volume gradient is largely abolished in supine CT and replaced by a ventrodorsal gradient. That is plausibly *why* nobody quantified an upper/lower vessel ratio on supine CT, and it means the candidate's cranial axis may be the wrong operationalization — the anterior–posterior axis should be treated as the leading alternative before any design freeze.
- **C2 got the only high-confidence verdict:** all three neighbors were read in full text, including a 2025 BMC Medical Imaging paper whose automated renal-surface-nodularity pipeline is exactly the measurement class C2 proposes (never connected to any model's prediction). Sole caveat: Merlin's Nature supplement was not inspected.
- **C1 and C4 share the same access limitation:** their closest methodological neighbor (RoentMod, npj Digital Medicine 2026 — counterfactual radiograph editing to expose model shortcuts) was paywalled, so non-overlap rests on abstracts and search summaries; both were therefore held at LIMITED_SEARCH rather than high confidence.

Per the charter's calibration rule, none of the NO_DUPLICATE verdicts constitute verified novelty — they record that a genuine multi-source search found distinguishable neighbors and no duplicate.
