Keystone screen complete for idea 032 — verdict **UNVERIFIABLE** (passes onward with the uncertainty recorded), written to `ideas/032/keystone_screen.md`.

What the investigation established, in three layers:

1. **Existence — verified.** The current Zenodo release states verbatim that it provides "the multi-labeled Circle of Willis anatomy generated with an automatic algorithm over CTA (cow-msk.nii.gz)" for the 149 public training cases, with instructions to cite the TopCoW paper. No QC or manual-review statement exists anywhere in the release.
2. **Taxonomy — verified at the schema level.** The cited TopCoW annotation scheme has 13 classes that separately label the Acom and both Pcoms — exactly the edges the idea needs to edit. I could not open an actual mask file to confirm the released files carry all 13 labels (raw downloads are approval-gated in this runner; the official archive is 99 GB), so that cheap direct check is flagged for the top of Stage 0.
3. **Fidelity — undocumented, and the published adjacent evidence is stratified adversely.** The updated TopCoW paper used ISLES'24 as an external CTA test set with >80% median Dice for top algorithms — but its validation cohort was "26 CTA patients whose CoW were not occluded within the ROI." That is the sharpest fact of the screen: the only published fidelity evidence for these algorithms on this cohort excluded exactly the occluded-CoW cases the idea's "remove the occluded edge" intervention depends on, and communicating arteries are the weakest classes even for expert annotators (76–89% Dice agreement vs ~90%+ elsewhere).

The residual-assumption check found the card's stated keystone is the right one (a rarity here), but added two obligations for Stage 0: mask QA must **stratify by occlusion location** rather than sampling 30 masks uniformly, and caliber stability must be assessed in NCCT space, since the masks ship resampled off the native CTA grid.

Nothing found demonstrates the keystone false, so this is not a KILL at screen prices — but the fidelity claim cannot be verified from any reachable primary source; it is only establishable by the Stage 0 census the card already plans.
