**Decision: ADVANCE TO REVISION** — with a fatal-as-written objection that is repairable without changing the question.

**What the review found.** The card proposes measuring bilateral MCA + basilar tortuosity in ISLES'24 and testing whether it explains a final-infarct model's prediction residuals. Verification against the challenge paper ([arXiv 2408.10966v2](https://arxiv.org/html/2408.10966v2)) established a fact the card never registered: every ISLES'24 patient is a thrombectomy-treated LVO case with TICI 2b/3 recanalization, imaged with the occlusion in place. That breaks the card three ways:

1. **The measurement is uncomputable as specified.** The cited tortuosity index is defined on the MCA M1 segment ([PMC5836575](https://pmc.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/articles/PMC5836575/)) — the modal occlusion site in this cohort, unopacified distal to the clot on the affected side.
2. **Inclusion selects against the exposure.** Tortuosity lowers the odds of achieving TICI ≥ 2b (a [2025 systematic review](https://www.sciencedirect.com/science/article/abs/pii/S0303846725003907) documents this: first-pass success 34% vs 59%), so conditioning on TICI 2b/3 compresses tortuosity variance and adds collider-shaped bias.
3. **The strongest rival mechanism is unnamed.** In a thrombectomy cohort, tortuosity plausibly causes larger infarcts through procedural difficulty — not "pressure history" — and the card's confound list misses it entirely.

I also flagged: the audited model is unspecified (released challenge artifacts trained on the public 149 cases would be leakage); the 10-GPU-hour compute claim contradicts nested-CV training; "narrow strata" over six covariates at n=149 is arithmetically empty; and the two-part endpoint reads "correctly used" as null, biasing the study toward a non-finding in exactly this cohort. The plain pitch passed the fidelity check. Suggested Mode C score drops from 3.9 to ~3.35 (identifiability 3→2, mechanism clarity 5→4).

**The constructive core:** a near-free decisive screen exists — compute patent-vessel tortuosity from the released CoW pseudolabels and occlusion masks and test it against the released age/hypertension variables. If tortuosity doesn't track age in this selected cohort, the vascular-age framing dies for ~zero cost before any model is touched.
