# Debate transcript

## Round 1 — CRITIC

**Position:** The proposed residual-association study does not answer the original question of whether the model *uses* tortuosity as a vascular-age and long-term pressure-load gauge.

**Argument:** The card's claimed endpoint is mechanistic use, but its smallest experiment is a conditional observational regression of prediction error on CTA-derived tortuosity in 149 thrombectomy-selected cases. Even after the critique's patent-vessel and procedural-covariate repairs, a coefficient can arise because tortuosity covaries with age, ancestry, atherosclerosis, collateral anatomy, CTA segmentation quality, or thrombectomy difficulty; a null can arise because conditioning on TICI 2b/3 restricts tortuosity variation. More importantly, neither result identifies model use, and the card itself admits that rung 1 requires a selective intervention or natural paired validation. Recasting the endpoint as “tortuosity-linked prediction bias” would make the experiment coherent, but it changes the deliverable from a use-and-mechanism claim to a bias audit and therefore fails the required identity check; that replacement belongs in a separate candidate. The cheap age/hypertension association screen is useful feasibility evidence for the proposed interpretation, but it also cannot bridge association to model use or establish long-term pressure load.

**What would change my mind:** Show a prespecified, credible selective intervention or natural paired design that changes patent-vessel tortuosity while holding lumen caliber, contrast filling, occlusion, perfusion, collateral topology, and other visible vascular-age cues fixed, produces a reproducible change in out-of-fold final-infarct predictions, and separately validates that the manipulated representation tracks age or pressure load rather than the named alternatives. Otherwise narrow the claim and register the bias audit as a successor.

**Status:** OPEN

## Round 1 — PROPOSER

**Responding to:** The observational residual study cannot answer the card's use-question in either direction; therefore the only coherent reading of the experiment is a bias audit, and a bias audit is a different deliverable — so the card either fails on identifiability or fails the claim-identity check.

**Response:** AMEND

**Argument:** One part of the objection is wrong and the rest is right, so I amend rather than rebut or concede outright.

The wrong part is the identity charge. The card never claims the regression establishes use: `rung_reached` is 0, `use_vs_association` says the conditional test "cannot prove use," and the deliverable sentence is the Mode C *target*, explicitly gated on "a credible selective tortuosity intervention or natural paired validation." Relabeling the screen a "bias audit" and then declaring the deliverable changed is the critic renaming my gate, not me changing my claim. Under the claim-identity rule (decision ledger, 2026-08-10), identity attaches to the deliverable sentence and the prohibited-conclusions set; the amendments below keep the sentence verbatim and only *grow* the prohibited set. The critique stage reached the same conclusion ("revision-in-place under the claim-identity rule").

The right part is that, as written, the card's positive arm was doing no work: a screen that cannot support the claim in the positive direction, attached to a rung-1 intervention that existed only as a phrase ("a later geometry-preserving counterfactual"), leaves the deliverable resting on nothing. The critic's "what would change my mind" is a fair demand for that missing design. Amendments:

1. **Adopt the critique's repairs wholesale** (they are prerequisites, not concessions to this round): patent-vessel TI with per-case segment sets determined from the released occlusion masks and reported exclusion rates; the thrombectomy-difficulty pathway named as the leading alternative, with adjustment for whatever of mTICI grade / pass count / procedure times Stage 0 finds in the released clinical table; primary observational readout = signed volume error regressed on patent-vessel TI with a prespecified covariate set and overlap diagnostics; the narrow-strata language and the "explains prediction residuals but not ground-truth residuals" pass/fail element dropped (the latter reported descriptively only).

2. **Reclassify the observational arms as kill-only gates.** The section-9 age/hypertension screen runs first; the residual regression second; neither carries any positive evidential weight toward the deliverable sentence, and the card will say so in `use_vs_association`. Their decisive value is exclusively negative: no age/hypertension tracking in this TICI-selected cohort, or no residual association, kills the card before any intervention is built. Added to prohibited conclusions: no vascular-age interpretation without procedural adjustment; no use claim from any observational arm.

3. **Specify the rung-1 design the critic demanded.** Prespecified peri-vascular tube-confined straightening counterfactual: extract the patent-vessel centerline; resample the CTA intensity field in a fixed-radius tube around it onto a smoothed centerline of reduced TI, preserving each cross-sectional intensity profile (lumen caliber and contrast filling carried unchanged); every voxel outside the tube, and every non-CTA input channel (perfusion maps, NCCT, occlusion mask), is bit-identical to the original. Dose–response over graded TI reduction; contralateral-edit control (straightening the healthy side should shift ipsilateral predictions less than affected-territory-relevant edits, sign prespecified at design time); in-distribution gates before any confirmatory readout — interpolation-artifact detector and a blinded real-vs-edited discrimination test, thresholds fixed in the feasibility memo, mirroring the edit-precedent requirement the ledger already imposes on idea 023. Failure of the realism gates kills rung 1 *within this card*; it does not license retreat to the observational arms as evidence.

What is lost, plainly: the card no longer promises a finding from its cheap experiments — it promises a kill-or-proceed decision, with all positive evidential weight concentrated in an editing technique that is unvalidated on CTA and may die at its own realism gates (vessel-adjacent tissue must be plausibly filled where the tube moves; that is a real failure mode, not a formality). Screen-stage identifiability stays at the critique's 2. And I concede the critic's last sentence fully: "long-term pressure-load gauge" can never be earned inside ISLES'24 alone — rung 3 requires external cohorts with independent age/pressure validation, which the card already states and the revision will keep prohibited until then.

**Status:** OPEN

## Round 2 — CRITIC

**Position:** The tube-confined straightening edit does not isolate tortuosity, because changing an intracranial artery's path necessarily changes its spatial relationship to branch origins and surrounding tissue, so a prediction response cannot be attributed specifically to winding geometry.

**Argument:** The amendment passes the identity check in form: it retains the original use claim, makes the observational analyses kill-only, and adds an intervention intended to support rung 1. But the proposed intervention does not satisfy the selectivity condition that would make rung 1 identifiable. Preserving cross-sectional CTA profiles preserves caliber and intraluminal contrast only in vessel-local coordinates. Mapping those profiles onto a smoother centerline relocates the lumen relative to brain tissue and to any daughter vessels that leave the edited segment. If daughter vessels remain bit-identical outside the tube, their junctions are stretched, kinked, duplicated, or disconnected; if they move with the parent, collateral topology and the visible vascular territory change. The fixed-radius tube also replaces tissue along the new path and fills tissue along the old path. These are not merely interpolation artifacts detectable by a generic real-versus-edited classifier: they are unavoidable anatomical consequences of changing centerline excess length while holding the surrounding anatomy fixed. A model response could therefore reflect branch-junction plausibility, vessel-to-territory registration, altered local vessel density, or the synthetic tissue seam rather than TI. The contralateral control does not resolve this, because the affected and contralateral arteries have different occlusion, collateral, and perfusion relevance; unequal responses are predicted by those rival cues even if the model never encodes tortuosity. Thus the amendment supplies a concrete intervention, but not yet the selective tortuosity intervention demanded in Round 1.

**What would change my mind:** Show, before model testing, a topology-preserving deformation with explicit branch-point constraints and bidirectional dose controls that changes TI by a prespecified amount while quantitative audits show unchanged lumen caliber and contrast, unchanged branch connectivity and endpoints, unchanged vessel-to-territory assignment, and no detectable old-path/new-path tissue seam; then show that matched sham deformations with the same displaced-voxel volume and interpolation burden but unchanged TI produce materially smaller prediction shifts. A natural paired acquisition or validated vascular phantom that varies tortuosity without these edit couplings would also settle the objection.

**Status:** OPEN

## Round 2 — PROPOSER

**Responding to:** The round-1 tube edit is not a selective tortuosity intervention: moving a centerline while the surroundings stay fixed forces a dilemma at every daughter-vessel junction (break it or drag it), replaces tissue along both the old and new paths, and so guarantees anatomical side-channels — junction implausibility, vessel-to-territory misregistration, tissue seams — that a model could respond to instead of TI. The contralateral control cannot separate these because the two sides differ in occlusion and perfusion relevance anyway.

**Response:** AMEND

**Argument:** The tube edit as I specified it is dead, and I will not defend it. The critic's decomposition is exact: bit-identity outside a fixed-radius tube and continuity of daughter vessels crossing its boundary are jointly unsatisfiable once the parent's path changes, and copy-then-fill tissue handling manufactures seams by construction. Conceding that specific design, I amend by adopting the construction the critic's own "what would change my mind" describes, made concrete enough to be auditable:

1. **Replace tube resampling with a compactly supported diffeomorphic warp.** The parent segment, daughter-vessel origins, and surrounding parenchyma inside the support all move together under one smooth invertible deformation; identity at the support boundary. This resolves the junction dilemma by construction (a diffeomorphism cannot disconnect, duplicate, or kink a connected structure) and eliminates seams by construction (no voxel content is created or deleted, only deformed — there is no old-path hole to fill). Near-rigidity is enforced in a peri-luminal collar (as-rigid-as-possible-style constraint) so cross-sections are carried, not sheared, with elastic falloff outside the collar.

2. **Audits before any model inference, with numeric thresholds frozen in the feasibility memo.** Per edited volume: lumen caliber profile measured post-edit (not assumed — Jacobian determinant within a stated tolerance of 1 inside the collar); intraluminal intensity profile unchanged within tolerance; branch connectivity verified on the post-edit segmentation; daughter-endpoint displacement reported against a prespecified cap; vessel-to-territory overlap change bounded; a trained real-vs-edited discriminator at a stated operating point. Any audit failure discards the volume; audit pass rates are themselves reported.

3. **Matched TI-neutral shams — the critic's key demand, and it is constructible exactly.** Rotating the plane of an existing bend about its local chord axis preserves centerline arc length and endpoint positions, hence preserves TI identically, while displacing a comparable voxel volume through the same warp-and-resample machinery; rotation angle tunes displaced volume to match the TI-changing edit per case. The rung-1 primary contrast becomes differential: prediction shift under TI-changing warps must exceed shift under displacement-matched TI-neutral shams, with bidirectional dose (graded straightening and graded added sinuosity, monotone signed response prespecified). If sham shifts are comparable to edit shifts, the model is responding to deformation per se and rung 1 fails inside this card — no retreat to the observational arms.

What is lost, and it is not small. First, attribution is now differential, not absolute: the claim rests on an effect-size gap between matched edit families, which multiplies the number of edited volumes (edit + sham + dose ladder, per case, per side) and invalidates the card's "2–3 weeks" intervention estimate; the feasibility memo must re-cost it honestly, and the idea-023 edit-precedent requirement (ledger 2026-08-17) applies with full force — no workable precedent for near-rigid vascular warps in CTA is a legitimate kill. Second, a residual coupling survives even this design: the diffeomorphism necessarily co-deforms parenchyma and daughter-vessel courses inside the support, and the sham matches the *amount* of deformation, not its specific correlation with straightening. This is the second amendment to the intervention arm in two rounds; the risk is now concentrated in one editing technique passing audits nobody has run on CTA.

That leaves one genuine crux, which I put to the critic plainly rather than paper over: is a sham-controlled differential with passing audits, bidirectional dose, and prespecified sign sufficient for rung 1, or does the objection demand absolute invariance of everything but TI? If the latter, no image-space intervention on real anatomy can ever meet it — TI is a property of an embedded curve, and changing an embedded curve changes its embedding — and the honest terminus for this card is PAUSE pending a natural paired acquisition or phantom, both of which lie outside ISLES'24 and the second of which would make the dataset no longer load-bearing. I hold that the differential standard is the one the ledger's rung-1 semantics actually use (idea 021's signed-conjunction design was accepted on exactly this discriminate-among-mechanisms logic, not on absolute invariance), so the amended design answers the round-1 demand. But if round 3 shows the sham construction is also broken — for instance, that chord-axis rotation systematically differs from straightening in some model-visible statistic other than TI — I will concede rather than amend a third time.

**Status:** OPEN

## Round 3 — CRITIC

**Position:** The diffeomorphic CTA edit still does not isolate tortuosity in a multimodal ISLES'24 model, because it changes vascular-to-tissue registration relative to the bit-identical NCCT and perfusion channels, and the proposed TI-neutral sham does not match that model-visible mismatch.

**Argument:** The amendment passes the identity check: if selective, a bidirectional TI intervention would still test whether the final-infarct model uses tortuosity, while external validation remains correctly required for the vascular-age/pressure-load interpretation. It also answers my prior topology-and-seam objection better than the tube edit: moving the CTA vessel, its branches, and adjacent CTA parenchyma under one invertible field can preserve within-CTA connectivity and avoid an old-path fill seam. But ISLES'24 prediction is multimodal. The amendment deforms only CTA while keeping perfusion maps and NCCT bit-identical. Consequently, every TI-changing warp relocates the artery and nearby CTA anatomy relative to the fixed hypoperfused territory and fixed tissue anatomy. A model can respond to that cross-modal displacement without encoding TI at all—for example, to the changed distance or orientation between an M1 course and its downstream Tmax/CBF deficit. The chord-axis-rotation sham is not an adequate control: matching total displaced-voxel volume and interpolation burden does not match the spatial displacement field relative to the perfusion lesion. Straightening and added-sinuosity systematically move vessel points toward or away from different tissue territories, whereas rotating a bend moves them around the chord; anisotropic, territory-dependent cross-modal mismatches therefore differ by construction. A larger edit-minus-sham response, even with monotone bidirectional dose and all proposed within-CTA audits passing, remains attributable to vascular-to-perfusion registration rather than tortuosity. This is not a demand for absolute invariance; it is a named alternative mechanism created specifically by the multimodal intervention and left unmatched by its control.

**What would change my mind:** Before model inference, show a control family whose deformation fields are matched to each TI edit not only on displaced volume but on the distribution of vessel-point displacement vectors, distances to the fixed perfusion-deficit boundary, and vessel-to-territory overlap, while preserving TI; then demonstrate that TI-changing responses exceed this matched null family. Alternatively, apply one common diffeomorphism to every spatial input, inverse-warp the output for comparison, and use TI-neutral common-warp shams matched on tissue deformation—provided a quantitative audit shows that the only systematic between-family geometric difference available to the model is TI. If neither construction is feasible, the rung-1 arm should pause for a natural paired acquisition rather than interpret edit responses as tortuosity use.

**Status:** OPEN

## Round 3 — PROPOSER

**Responding to:** Because ISLES'24 prediction is multimodal and my round-2 warp deforms only the CTA channel, every TI-changing edit displaces the artery relative to the bit-identical perfusion and NCCT channels; the chord-axis sham matches how much tissue is displaced but not where the vessel moves relative to the fixed perfusion deficit — straightening carries a systematic radial component toward the bend chord, and hence toward or away from the hypoperfused territory, while rotation moves points azimuthally around it — so a larger edit-minus-sham response remains attributable to vascular-to-perfusion misregistration rather than tortuosity use.

**Response:** CONCEDE

**Argument:** The objection is correct, and it lands on the exact tripwire I set in round 2: "if round 3 shows the sham construction is also broken — for instance, that chord-axis rotation systematically differs from straightening in some model-visible statistic other than TI — I will concede rather than amend a third time." That is precisely what round 3 shows. The distribution of vessel-point displacements relative to the fixed perfusion-deficit boundary is a model-visible statistic other than TI, and it differs between the edit family and the sham family by construction, not by a fixable parameter choice: straightening is radial with respect to each bend's chord, rotation is azimuthal, and no tuning of rotation angle equalizes the two fields' relationship to a fixed extravascular reference. The flaw attaches to every CTA-only deformation, so it kills the family, not just the instance.

On the critic's two constructive escapes, assessed rather than adopted. The displacement-matched TI-neutral family is not certifiable: matching displaced volume plus any finite list of named statistics cannot establish that "the only systematic between-family geometric difference available to the model is TI" — the guarantee is only as strong as the statistic list, and the cross-modal mismatch channel stays open in both families with different fine structure. I cannot exhibit such a construction and will not claim one. The common-warp equivariance design — one diffeomorphism applied to every spatial input, inverse-warped output, deformation-matched TI-neutral common-warp shams — is coherent, removes the cross-modal channel by construction, and I cannot break it in this round. But it is a materially different experiment: the estimand becomes TI-linked non-equivariance versus deformation-matched non-equivariance, it needs a volume-preservation constraint to protect lesion volume in the warped perfusion channels, and the audit it requires has no precedent on CTA or any other modality. Adopting it now would be the third redesign of the intervention arm in three rounds — the decorate-don't-kill pattern the rules and the ledger both warn against (idea 003 died exactly this way; three amendments usually means the original design was wrong). I keep the round-2 commitment and concede instead.

What survives, and the disposition I recommend to the verdict stage. The observational arms stand as ratified in critique — kill-only, cheap, public-data-only: patent-vessel TI against released age and hypertension first, the signed-volume-error regression second. Their decisive value is purely negative and is untouched by this concession; they can still kill the card for nearly nothing. The rung-1 arm has no valid design inside this card: the tube edit died in round 2 on junction-and-seam grounds, and the CTA-only diffeomorphism with chord-axis sham dies here on cross-modal grounds. The critic's common-warp construction is the one candidate unblock that preserves the deliverable sentence verbatim; in claim-identity terms a future pursuit of it is a revision of this card, but it must enter through a fresh feasibility review carrying the edit-precedent requirement (ledger 2026-08-17), not through a debate-round patch. The honest terminus now is PAUSE: run the two kill-only screens if the verdict stage wants them, and gate any intervention on either a validated common-warp equivariance protocol or a natural paired acquisition.

No spin-off is registered: the common-warp design answers the same one-sentence question, so it is an unblock path for this card, not a new candidate.

**Status:** CONCEDED
