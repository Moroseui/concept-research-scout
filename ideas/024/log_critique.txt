Critique written to `ideas/024/critique.md`. **Decision: REJECT** — the research turned up two independently fatal, primary-source-verified problems, either of which would kill the candidate alone.

**Fatal objection A — no subject.** The card's smallest decisive experiment says "freeze one already trained raw-CTP model," but no such model is publicly obtainable. Of the twelve ISLES'24 finalists, only three consumed raw 4D CTP, and none released weights (I had the repos fetched and checked — Ninjas and MIPLAB-PrediCTP are code-only; HSK-CoreFinders has no repo). The only teams with downloadable weights use derived perfusion maps + CTA. Worse, the premise inverts: the challenge paper itself states map-based models "consistently outperform those trained directly on raw 4D CTP series," and the one published raw-CTP result on ISLES'24 reports Dice 0.20. Auditing a self-trained weak model for a second-moment signal makes any null uninterpretable.

**Fatal objection B — no instrument.** The proposed X-measurement (variance of the residue function from nonparametric delay-insensitive deconvolution) was explicitly considered and rejected — for CT by name — by Mouridsen, Hansen, and Østergaard, the originators of the CTH construct (JCBFM 2014): SVD-family oscillations propagate into dR/dt, and the estimator's bias is SNR-independent, meaning the card's stability-across-regularizers gate is structurally blind to it (stable-but-invalid passes). I also found the card's anchor precedent, Potreck 2019, is misattributed: it's a DSC-MRI study using the parametric Bayesian pipeline, not CT perfusion. And macrovascular bolus dispersion masquerades as transit-time heterogeneity (Willats/Calamante), which makes the "capillary" claim in the deliverable sentence unreachable on LVO data regardless of estimator — a claim-identity change, so not repairable in place under the 2026-08-10 governance rule.

The critique also records this as the **fourth occurrence of the wrong-keystone error**: the keystone screen verified the nearest checkable thing (4D CTP exists at 1 Hz) while the load-bearing facts — an auditable model exists, and the estimator is valid — were checkable today and are both adverse.

The constructive close is deliberately negative: the only low-hanging fruit (auditing the released map-based winners) is already occupied by shortlisted ideas 021 and 023, and no CTH-flavored easier version survives, so I recommended against registering a successor. The file is left uncommitted for the driver, per the repo's stage convention.
