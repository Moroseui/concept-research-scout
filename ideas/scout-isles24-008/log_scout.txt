**The five candidates, ranked by priority:**

1. **c02 (Mode B, 4.05) — The unpredictable infarct.** Ground truth is drawn on 2–9-day post-thrombectomy MRI, so it can contain procedural and new-territory infarcts no baseline image could predict. A model-free geometry census measures how much GT lies in disconnected components in distant arterial territories, outside any baseline hypoperfusion — either certifying the benchmark or quantifying an irreducible error floor that partly explains the low leaderboard ceiling. Decisive both ways, roughly a week of work, no training needed.
2. **c04 (Mode C, 3.75) — The blood arrives backwards.** Is the model reading the *direction* of bolus arrival in the Tmax field (surface-inward = retrograde collateral filling, per Menon's multiphase-CTA construct) as a collateral pressure gauge? Honest keystone NOT_INSPECTED: Tmax quantization could make gradients unmeasurable; a one-afternoon audit gates everything.
3. **c01 (Mode A, 3.70) — The ghost core and the water clock.** The NCCT-only winner never sees the perfusion maps; the ghost-infarct-core literature (Boned 2017, Ballout 2023) says CTP threshold cores lie in exactly this early-imaged, reperfused population. Question: is the model's disagreement with the threshold core organized by net water uptake (Broocks, Stroke 2018) at matched perfusion? Label-free primary readout, cross-model-disagreement template.
4. **c05 (Mode C, 3.35) — The rough edge grows.** Viscous-fingering analogy: a fingered deficit boundary as the signature of collateral supply at its margin. Identifiability scored honestly low (2) and the card flags its own seductive imagery per the style rule; a two-day measurability gate kills it cheaply.
5. **c03 (Mode B, 3.30) — Written off by depth.** Does the model triage by depth beneath the pial surface (the internal borderzone) at matched perfusion? Explicitly differentiated from killed idea-038 by within-patient matched-cell design.
