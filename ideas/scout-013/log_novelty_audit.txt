Three findings from the search matter beyond the verdicts:

1. **C2's card is stale.** An interventional attribution audit of Sybil already exists — *Auditing Sybil*, arXiv:2602.02560, ICML 2026 (read at full text). It never tests fissures, so C2 survives as novel, but it found Sybil behaves roughly as a nodule-linear model with an age/bone-density baseline, which is adverse prior evidence against an independent fissure signal and also supplies reusable intervention machinery. Any revision must engage it.
2. **C5's novelty is certified but its physics got weaker.** Every existing venous-asymmetry/collateral result rides on SWI deoxyhemoglobin susceptibility or CTA contrast opacification — mechanisms absent on non-contrast CT. The unoccupied niche may be unoccupied because the signal doesn't exist there, which makes the card's model-free Phase-1 gate load-bearing (consistent with its "sounds probably wrong" self-label).
3. **C3 gains prior legwork.** Lin et al. 2024 (J Med Syst, DOI 10.1007/s10916-023-02030-2) demonstrated a CXR-derived osteoporosis signal independently stratifies mortality (HR 1.67), so the skeletal-frailty premise is now demonstrated rather than hypothesized; the unresolved risk remains anchor-model access, as the card already says.
