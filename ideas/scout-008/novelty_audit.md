# Novelty audit — scouting cycle 008

## C1 — The cirrhosis model may be reading the bumpiness of the liver's edge (baseline)

1. **Neighbors.** Smith et al., *Liver Surface Nodularity Quantification from Routine CT Images as a Biomarker for Detection and Evaluation of Cirrhosis*, Radiology 2016, DOI `10.1148/radiol.2016151542`: defined a computer-derived LSN score and showed reproducible cirrhosis discrimination. Lo et al., *Feasibility and reproducibility of liver surface nodularity quantification for the assessment of liver cirrhosis using CT and MRI*, Eur J Radiol Open 2017, DOI `10.1016/j.ejro.2017.07.001`: tested cross-observer and cross-modality reproducibility of LSN. Liu et al., *Using Artificial Intelligence to Predict Cirrhosis From Computed Tomography Scans*, Hepatology Communications 2023, PMCID `PMC10584300`: built a cirrhosis classifier whose engineered CT morphomics included an explicit liver-surface nodularity measure.
2. **Delta.** C1 asks whether a separately trained, general-purpose Merlin representation *uses* LSN through a selective intervention; the closest AI neighbor put nodularity into its feature set by design, so this is a model-decoding delta rather than a new cirrhosis biomarker.
3. **Why not done — `NEW_CAPABILITY`.** Merlin (`arXiv:2406.06512`; DOI `10.1038/s41586-026-10181-8`) only recently supplied a released 3D abdominal CT foundation model with hundreds of phenotype outputs that can be interrogated independently of an LSN-specific training pipeline.
4. **Verdict — `INCREMENTAL`.** The search found no Merlin-LSN reliance test, but explicit nodularity-based AI cirrhosis prediction is already published; novelty is confined to testing whether an unguided foundation model learned and uses that known feature.

## C2 — The chest-CT model may see the heart by watching the airway splay (baseline)

1. **Neighbors.** Karabulut, *CT assessment of tracheal carinal angle and its determinants*, Br J Radiol 2005, DOI `10.1259/bjr/75107416`: quantified the CT carinal angle and evaluated left-atrial size, habitus, thoracic dimensions, and carinal position as determinants. Murray et al., *Widening of the tracheal bifurcation on chest radiographs: value as a sign of left atrial enlargement*, AJR 1995, DOI `10.2214/ajr.164.5.7717208`: found poor correlation and weak diagnostic performance for carinal angles as signs of left-atrial enlargement. Quinton et al., *The reliability of chest radiographs in predicting left atrial enlargement*, Cardiovasc J Afr 2010, DOI `10.5830/CVJA-2010-008`: found subcarinal angle measurement repeatable but inaccurate for predicting left-atrial enlargement.
2. **Delta.** C2 does not propose the angle as a diagnostic test; it asks whether CT-CLIP uses this weak, indirect airway sign for its cardiomegaly output after separating it from gross heart width and habitus.
3. **Why not done — `NEW_CAPABILITY`.** The released CT-CLIP/CT-RATE system (`arXiv:2403.17834`) recently made a frozen 3D chest-CT cardiomegaly readout and its internal representation available for this specific audit.
4. **Verdict — `NO_DUPLICATE_FOUND_LIMITED_SEARCH`.** Searches spanning the anatomical sign, cardiomegaly AI, CT-CLIP, and representation attribution found no model-use test, but the search was bibliographic/abstract-level and cannot establish a verified gap.

## C3 — The model that 'predicts a blood count' may just be reading how bright the blood is (baseline)

1. **Neighbors.** Collins et al., *Prediction of anemia on unenhanced computed tomography of the thorax*, J Comput Assist Tomogr 2003, PMID `12625080`: established unenhanced blood-pool attenuation and classic CT signs as anemia predictors. Boos et al., *Detection of Low Blood Hemoglobin Levels on Pulmonary CT Angiography: A Feasibility Study Combining Dual-Energy CT and Machine Learning*, Diagnostics 2023, DOI `10.3390/diagnostics13162702`, PMCID `PMC10459752`: trained an XGBoost anemia detector using virtual-noncontrast blood attenuation, with descending-aortic attenuation most important. Kopp et al., *Anemia Detection by Hemoglobin Quantification on Contrast-enhanced Photon-counting CT Data Sets*, Radiology 2022, DOI `10.1148/radiol.220063`: derived hemoglobin/anemia information from spectral contrast-enhanced CT blood-pool measurements.
2. **Delta.** C3's delta is the within-patient perturbation of a pre-existing foundation-model anemia output by ordinary iodinated contrast to test *reliance* on blood HU; prior work intentionally built anemia predictors from blood attenuation and therefore did not audit a model that ostensibly learned the cue without being told.
3. **Why not done — `NEW_CAPABILITY`.** A recent general-purpose abdominal CT model such as Merlin (`arXiv:2406.06512`) could expose an independently learned anemia phenotype score; without such an output there is no claimed black-box behavior to decode.
4. **Verdict — `INCREMENTAL`.** Machine-learning anemia prediction from blood attenuation already exists. The paired contrast stress test is a useful new validation design if the Merlin output and cohort exist, but it narrows the claim to implicit reliance rather than discovering a new physical cue.

## C4 — The emphysema call may read the shape of the holes, not just how many (baseline)

1. **Neighbors.** Mishima et al., *Complexity of terminal airspace geometry assessed by lung computed tomography in normal subjects and patients with chronic obstructive pulmonary disease*, PNAS 1999, DOI `10.1073/pnas.96.16.8829`: introduced the power-law exponent of low-attenuation cluster sizes as a CT measure of terminal-airspace complexity. Tanabe et al., *Low morphometric complexity of emphysematous lesions predicts survival in patients with chronic obstructive pulmonary disease*, Eur Radiol 2018, DOI `10.1007/s00330-018-5551-7`, PMID `29959456`: showed extent-independent prognostic value for cluster-size and box-counting dimensions. Suki et al., *CT Imaging-Based Low-Attenuation Super Clusters in Three Dimensions and the Progression of Emphysema*, Chest 2019, DOI `10.1016/j.chest.2018.09.009`, PMCID `PMC6344385`: described 3D coalescing super-clusters and a biomechanical progression mechanism.
2. **Delta.** C4 asks whether CT-CLIP's emphysema score causally depends on the extent-orthogonalized cluster-complexity signal; the neighbors establish the morphometry and biology but do not decode a foundation model.
3. **Why not done — `NEW_CAPABILITY`.** CT-CLIP and CT-RATE (`arXiv:2403.17834`) recently provided a released volumetric representation with an explicit emphysema text readout on which complexity-versus-extent reliance can be tested.
4. **Verdict — `NO_DUPLICATE_FOUND_LIMITED_SEARCH`.** No CT-CLIP or other foundation-model study testing emphysema cluster complexity versus extent was found; because the audit relied largely on searchable full text and abstracts rather than a systematic database review, this is not verified novelty.

## C5 — The lung-cancer model may read the aorta as an ageing clock (baseline)

1. **Neighbors.** Mikhael et al., *Sybil: A Validated Deep Learning Model to Predict Future Lung Cancer Risk From a Single Low-Dose Chest Computed Tomography*, J Clin Oncol 2023, DOI `10.1200/JCO.22.01345`: introduced and externally validated Sybil and reported spatial localization analyses, but not aortic tortuosity. Sobieski et al., *Auditing Sybil: Explaining Deep Lung Cancer Risk Prediction Through Generative Interventional Attributions*, `arXiv:2602.02560`: performed generative object-level interventions on Sybil and reported nodule behavior, artifact sensitivity, and radial bias; the abstract did not identify aortic tortuosity. Belvroy et al., *Tortuosity of the descending thoracic aorta: Normal values by age*, PLOS ONE 2019, DOI `10.1371/journal.pone.0215549`: defined CT centerline tortuosity norms and quantified their age dependence.
2. **Delta.** C5 targets one named extra-pulmonary geometry—centerline arc-to-chord aortic tortuosity—and proposes age/emphysema/calcification-controlled intervention; the closest Sybil audit is methodologically very close but, on accessible evidence, did not test this quantity.
3. **Why not done — `NEW_CAPABILITY`.** Released Sybil weights plus the 2026 S(H)NAP-style 3D generative intervention framework (`arXiv:2602.02560`) newly make an object-specific causal test plausible rather than merely correlating aortic shape with risk.
4. **Verdict — `NO_DUPLICATE_FOUND_LIMITED_SEARCH`.** No duplicate was found, but the closest 2026 paper could only be checked through its abstract/searchable summary here; its full attribution inventory must be inspected before advancement.
