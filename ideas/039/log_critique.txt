Critique complete: `ideas/039/critique.md` written, decision **ADVANCE TO REVISION** — no fatal, unrepairable objection, but the card as written cannot support its own deliverable sentence.

**The decisive objection (header item):** the card's use-versus-association design — remove the central-volume residual via manifold projection, compare against equal-energy tangent shams — identifies only that the model is *directionally sensitive* to the off-manifold component. Any nonlinear (even linear) fusion responds anisotropically to spatially structured edits, so a model with no inconsistency computation at all can pass every stated gate, including monotone dose response. The deliverable sentence's "hidden confidence map" is an *interaction* claim (high residual attenuates the weight of perfusion evidence), and nothing in the design measures that interaction. The repair keeps the question intact: add a confirmatory arm applying an identical small perfusion-evidence edit at severity-matched high-X vs low-X sites and test for attenuated response. That stays revision-in-place under the claim-identity rule.

**Other findings worth knowing:**

- **Triviality risk is elevated by the card's own citation.** Konstas et al. (the card's invariant source) notes MTT is often *calculated from* CBV/CBF — if icobrain cva stores the quotient, the residual is pure quantization and the idea dies at Stage 0. I could not find public documentation of icobrain's MTT derivation, so this stays a Stage 0 question, but I required an explicit algebraic-dependence regression test, not just the rank-stability check.
- **A confound the card missed:** editing CBF/CBV/MTT while leaving Tmax untouched *introduces* a novel MTT–Tmax incoherence the model could be reacting to instead.
- **Citations verified exactly** (Kudo: DOI 10.1148/radiol.254082000, PMID 20032153; ISP-Net: CMPB 215:106630; Konstas: PMID 19270105). Adversarial novelty search found only physics-informed-NN work using conservation laws as fitting constraints — the inverse direction — so the gap survives at limited-search confidence.
- **The "final-infarct model" will in practice be a self-trained 2D surrogate:** the ISLES'24 winner (arXiv 2505.18424, test Dice 28.5 ± 21.27) has no located public checkpoint. That makes the Dice ≥ 0.20 gate defensible but requires "a representative surrogate" wording plus a new channel-reliance gate (a surrogate ignoring CBV or MTT makes every null uninterpretable).
- **Plain-pitch fidelity: two named defects** — the pitch states local identity violation as fact when it is the unverified keystone, and "the prediction model" implies an existing clinical/winning model.
- **Data readiness is overstated:** Stage 0 requires the full 99 GB monolithic `train.7z`; I recommend scoring data readiness 3, not 4.

The constructive close says plainly this is worth doing: Stage 0 has a guaranteed floor, since whether ISLES'24's released MTT is algebraically dependent on CBF/CBV (three channels, two degrees of freedom) is a publishable dataset-composition fact either way — kept inside this card, not spun off, to avoid spending stages twice.
