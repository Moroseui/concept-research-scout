# Novelty audit — scouting cycle 016

## C1 — Name the noise the kernel writes: the mediator behind idea 004's reconstruction shifts (baseline)

1. **Neighbors.** (i) Shafiq-ul-Hassan et al., “Accounting for reconstruction kernel-induced variability in CT radiomic features using noise power spectra,” *Journal of Medical Imaging* 2018, DOI `10.1117/1.JMI.5.1.011013`: measured 3-D noise power spectra and used NPS peak frequency to correct kernel-induced radiomic-feature variability. (ii) Choe et al., “Deep Learning–based Image Conversion of CT Reconstruction Kernels Improves Radiomics Reproducibility for Pulmonary Nodules or Masses,” *Radiology* 2019, DOI `10.1148/radiol.2019181960`: learned paired sharp-to-soft/soft-to-sharp kernel conversion and evaluated radiomic reproducibility. (iii) “Deep learning-based harmonization of CT reconstruction kernels towards improved clinical task performance,” *European Radiology* 2023, PMID `36355196`: converted B30f/B70f images and tested downstream radiomics classifiers for lung-cancer-versus-tuberculosis and nodal-metastasis tasks.
2. **Delta.** The candidate uses already-ratified, per-head output shifts from the same acquisition and asks whether measured high-frequency noise is their mediator, then separates noise injection from resolution filtering; the neighbors harmonized images or features but did not causally decompose a frozen multi-abnormality classifier's paired score shifts.
3. **Why not done.** `NEW_CAPABILITY` — the frozen idea-004 manifest, 7,650 joined per-pair/per-head score differences, deterministic CT-CLIP inference stack, and released ClassFine checkpoint create a behavior-specific target that did not previously exist.
4. **Verdict.** `NO_DUPLICATE_FOUND_HIGH_CONFIDENCE`. Searches across PubMed/PMC, DOI indexes, and arXiv found extensive NPS and kernel-harmonization work but no study joining natural paired score shifts to band-resolved mediation plus split physical interventions. This does not establish novelty beyond the searched record.

## C2 — The mortality model is wearing the patient's hardware (baseline)

1. **Neighbors.** (i) Lu et al., “Deep Learning to Assess Long-term Mortality From Chest Radiographs,” *JAMA Network Open* 2019, DOI `10.1001/jamanetworkopen.2019.7416`, PMID `31322692`: developed CXR-risk and used Grad-CAM to localize prognostic image regions. (ii) “Deep learning to predict long-term mortality from plain chest X-ray in patients referred for suspected coronary artery disease,” *Journal of Thoracic Disease* 2024, PMCID `PMC11388213`: predicted six-year mortality and reported attention around the cardiac silhouette, hila, aorta, clavicles, and other regions. (iii) “Deep Learning-Based Algorithm for the Detection and Characterization of MRI Safety of Cardiac Implantable Electronic Devices on Chest Radiographs,” 2021, PMCID `PMC8546129`: detected and characterized pacemakers/defibrillators on radiographs, establishing that X is machine-measurable.
2. **Delta.** The candidate removes detected cardiac hardware within the same radiograph and compares mortality-score changes with sham and noncardiac-hardware controls; the mortality papers only localized broadly, while the device paper performed identification rather than prognosis-model intervention.
3. **Why not done.** `BLIND_SPOT` — prognosis-model interpretation has been framed around latent anatomy and disease burden, while cardiac-device research is framed around identification and MRI safety; the disciplinary split left visible treated-disease proxies untested as causal inputs.
4. **Verdict.** `NO_DUPLICATE_FOUND_HIGH_CONFIDENCE`. Multi-source searches for mortality/risk models combined with pacemaker, implanted device, hardware, saliency, ablation, or removal found no device-intervention study. The proposed checkpoint-access gate remains separate from novelty.

## C3 — The fat inside the silhouette: epicardial adipose in the cardiomegaly score (baseline)

1. **Neighbors.** (i) Commandeur et al., “Deep Learning for Quantification of Epicardial and Thoracic Adipose Tissue From Non-Contrast CT,” *IEEE Transactions on Medical Imaging* 2018, DOI `10.1109/TMI.2018.2804799`, PMID `29994362`: automatically segmented and quantified epicardial and thoracic fat. (ii) Commandeur et al., “Fully Automated CT Quantification of Epicardial Adipose Tissue by Deep Learning: A Multicenter Study,” *Radiology: Artificial Intelligence* 2019, DOI `10.1148/ryai.2019190045`: validated automated EAT quantification across centers. (iii) Paling and Williamson, “Epipericardial fat pad: CT findings,” *Radiology* 1987, DOI `10.1148/radiology.165.2.3659352`, PMID `3659352`: showed that cardiac fat pads can alter the apparent cardiac contour and sometimes mimic cardiomegaly on radiographs.
2. **Delta.** The candidate asks whether a frozen 3-D CT cardiomegaly head uses EAT at fixed chamber geometry and tests that with an attenuation-only substitution; the neighbors measure EAT or document a projectional mimic but do not interrogate a cardiomegaly classifier.
3. **Why not done.** `NEW_CAPABILITY` — a released 18-head volumetric chest-CT model with a verified deterministic inference pipeline and a named cardiomegaly output makes tissue-specific counterfactual testing practical.
4. **Verdict.** `NO_DUPLICATE_FOUND_HIGH_CONFIDENCE`. Searches spanning EAT segmentation, cardiomegaly mimics, chest-CT classifiers, and counterfactual interpretation found no direct duplicate. The exact delta is model-use attribution, not discovery that fat affects the cardiac silhouette.

## C4 — The skeleton's tree rings: Harris lines inside the bone-age model (baseline)

1. **Neighbors.** (i) Suter et al., “Technical note: standardized and semiautomated Harris lines detection,” *American Journal of Physical Anthropology* 2008, DOI `10.1002/ajpa.20901`, PMID `18711729`: implemented semiautomated radiographic Harris-line detection and formation-age estimation. (ii) Kitamura and Pan, “Artificial Intelligence Class Activation Mapping of Bone Age,” *Radiology* 2022, DOI `10.1148/radiol.211790`: mapped the regions consistently used by a bone-age model but did not intervene on named findings. (iii) “Probing an AI regression model for hand bone age determination using gradient-based saliency mapping,” 2021, PMCID `PMC8134559`: applied black-box gradient saliency to an RSNA hand-radiograph bone-age model.
2. **Delta.** The candidate detects a named transverse metaphyseal finding and suppresses it against matched density-gradient shams to measure paired age-output change; prior work detected Harris lines or mapped bone-age attention, but did not connect the two with an intervention.
3. **Why not done.** `BLIND_SPOT` — Harris-line measurement has lived largely in paleopathology/anthropology, whereas bone-age AI interpretation has focused on anatomic regions and benchmark accuracy rather than incidental stress-history signs.
4. **Verdict.** `NO_DUPLICATE_FOUND_HIGH_CONFIDENCE`. Searches across medical, anthropological, and computer-vision sources found both halves of the proposed connection but no Harris-line intervention on a bone-age model. The clinical naming claim still depends on validating that automatically detected bands are true Harris lines.

## C5 — The cage remembers the hyperinflation: barrel chest inside the emphysema score (baseline)

1. **Neighbors.** (i) Sverzellati et al., “Computed Tomography Measurement of Rib Cage Morphometry in Emphysema,” *PLOS ONE* 2013, PMCID `PMC3729561`: quantified rib-cage diameters and areas in 816 smokers and modeled their relationship to emphysema and anthropometrics. (ii) Salito et al., “Alterations of diaphragm and rib cage morphometry in severe COPD patients by CT analysis,” *EMBC* 2015, DOI `10.1109/EMBC.2015.7319855`, PMID `26737755`: automatically reconstructed diaphragm/rib-cage geometry at total lung capacity and residual volume. (iii) “Deep Learning Enables Automatic Classification of Emphysema Pattern at CT,” *Radiology* 2020, PMCID `PMC6996603`: trained a deep network to reproduce Fleischner emphysema grades and related predictions to clinical outcomes.
2. **Delta.** The candidate tests whether cage geometry is conditionally encoded beyond LAA-950 and lung volume and whether erasing that feature subspace selectively changes a released emphysema score; prior morphometry established the cage-disease association, and prior classifiers predicted emphysema, but neither established model use of the cage.
3. **Why not done.** `BLIND_SPOT` — emphysema AI has been framed as parenchymal quantification or visual-pattern replication, so extra-pulmonary skeletal morphology was treated as clinical context/confounding rather than a candidate readout signal.
4. **Verdict.** `INCREMENTAL`. The precise representation-use experiment was not found, but the biological X, its CT measurement, and its emphysema association are established; novelty is confined to attributing an existing classifier's behavior. The conditional-separability and erasure design is scientifically useful but not a new mechanism.
