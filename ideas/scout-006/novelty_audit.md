# Novelty audit — scouting cycle 006

## Two papers say Sybil's residual is the background and name emphysema; neither measured the heart calcium sitting in the same scan — baseline

1. **Neighbors.** (i) Mikhael et al., “Sybil: A Validated Deep Learning Model to Predict Future Lung Cancer Risk From a Single Low-Dose Chest Computed Tomography,” *Journal of Clinical Oncology* 2023, [DOI 10.1200/JCO.22.01345; PMID 36634294](https://pmc.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/articles/PMC10419602/): established Sybil and quantified its residual performance when visible nodules at the future cancer site were removed, but did not quantify CAC. (ii) Sobieski et al., “Auditing Sybil: Explaining Deep Lung Cancer Risk Prediction Through Generative Interventional Attributions,” [arXiv:2602.02560](https://arxiv.org/abs/2602.02560): intervened on pulmonary nodules and identified non-nodule/background contributions and artifacts, without measuring coronary calcium. (iii) Zollinger et al., “Adapting Coronary CT for Lung Cancer Risk Prediction Using a Radiomic Deep Learning Model,” AATS 106th Annual Meeting 2026, abstract P205 (exact title/venue/year; [primary conference abstract](https://www.aats.org/resources/adapting-coronary-ct-for-lung--12651)): ran Sybil on noncontrast coronary CT and related its score to lung-cancer risk and emphysema, but did not test whether CAC explains the score.
2. **Delta.** The candidate uniquely places automated CAC, emphysema, and pulmonary-vessel measurements beside Sybil on the same nodule-free screening scans to estimate CAC's conditional contribution; this is a mechanism audit, not merely another validation cohort or a test of Sybil on cardiac CT.
3. **Why not done — `NEW_CAPABILITY`.** The openly released AI-CAC nongated-chest-CT scorer from Hagopian et al. (NEJM AI 2025, [DOI 10.1056/AIoa2400937; PMID 40746702](https://pubmed.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/40746702/)) makes scan-level CAC measurement at NLST scale newly practical alongside open Sybil inference.
4. **Verdict — `NOVEL_UNVERIFIED`.** No searched primary work directly tested whether CAC explains Sybil's score, but the 2026 coronary-CT Sybil study is extremely close and only an abstract was available; the exact covariates and any unreported CAC analysis require full-text or author verification.

## CT-CLIP has two calcification labels - coronary and arterial - so it can be asked whether it localises calcium or just sees hyperdensity — baseline

1. **Neighbors.** (i) Hamamci et al., “Developing Generalist Foundation Models from a Multimodal Dataset for 3D Computed Tomography,” [arXiv:2403.17834](https://arxiv.org/abs/2403.17834): introduced CT-RATE/CT-CLIP and separate coronary-artery-wall and arterial-wall calcification labels, but evaluated them against report-derived labels rather than continuous, anatomy-specific calcium burdens. (ii) van Velzen et al., “Automatic calcium scoring in low-dose chest CT using deep neural networks with dilated convolutions,” [arXiv:1711.00349](https://arxiv.org/abs/1711.00349): automatically detected and separately quantified coronary, thoracic-aortic, and valvular calcium on low-dose chest CT. (iii) Winkel et al., “Automatic coronary calcium scoring in chest CT using a deep neural network in direct comparison with non-contrast cardiac CT: A validation study,” *European Journal of Radiology* 2021, [DOI 10.1016/j.ejrad.2020.109428; PMID 33285350](https://pubmed.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/33285350/): validated automated coronary calcium quantification on routine chest CT against dedicated cardiac CT and expert assessment.
2. **Delta.** Rather than building or validating another calcium detector, the candidate audits whether a generalist model's two report-trained output heads quantitatively and anatomically dissociate coronary from aortic calcium on the same volumes.
3. **Why not done — `NEW_CAPABILITY`.** CT-CLIP/CT-RATE ([arXiv:2403.17834](https://arxiv.org/abs/2403.17834)) newly supplies one frozen model with both anatomically distinct output scores and its evaluation volumes; older calcium work supplied measurements but no such foundation-model semantics to audit.
4. **Verdict — `NOVEL_UNVERIFIED`.** The searched works do not perform this CT-CLIP double dissociation, but the search cannot establish absence across the rapidly moving 2025–2026 CT-foundation-model literature.

## An abdominal foundation model predicts diabetes - ask whether it is quietly reading the fat in the liver — baseline

1. **Neighbors.** (i) Tallam et al., “Fully Automated Abdominal CT Biomarkers for Type 2 Diabetes Using Deep Learning,” *Radiology* 2022, [DOI 10.1148/radiol.211914; PMID 35380492](https://pmc.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/articles/PMC9270681/): predicted/associated type 2 diabetes with automated abdominal CT biomarkers, and its selected multivariable predictors explicitly included average liver attenuation alongside pancreatic, visceral-fat, plaque, and BMI measures. (ii) Tran et al., “Hepatic and abdominal adiposity in type 2 diabetes as assessed with machine learning on computed tomography scans,” *Diabetes, Obesity and Metabolism* 2026, [DOI 10.1111/dom.70557; PMID 41705581](https://pubmed.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/41705581/): quantified spleen–liver attenuation difference and abdominal adiposity and related those phenotypes to type 2 diabetes. (iii) Blankemeier et al., “Merlin: A Vision Language Foundation Model for 3D Computed Tomography,” [arXiv:2406.06512](https://arxiv.org/abs/2406.06512): introduced Merlin and its diabetes prediction capability but did not decompose that output into liver, pancreas, and visceral-fat cues.
2. **Delta.** The only strong delta is model auditing: it asks whether an already-trained Merlin diabetes score depends uniquely on liver attenuation after competing abdominal phenotypes are controlled; liver attenuation as a diabetes biomarker is established, so presenting that biological connection itself as new would be incorrect.
3. **Why not done — `NEW_CAPABILITY`.** The released Merlin model and continuous diabetes output ([arXiv:2406.06512](https://arxiv.org/abs/2406.06512)) newly make it possible to audit a general abdominal foundation model rather than train a bespoke biomarker classifier.
4. **Verdict — `INCREMENTAL`.** Tallam et al. already put automated liver attenuation into a multivariable CT diabetes model. Probing Merlin rather than a handcrafted phenotype model is a real but narrow interpretability delta, not a new imaging–disease discovery.

## Merlin predicts osteoporosis - ask whether it reads the density of the bone or the shape of a column that has begun to buckle — baseline

1. **Neighbors.** (i) Burns et al., “Vertebral Body Compression Fractures and Bone Density: Automated Detection and Classification on CT Images,” *Radiology* 2017, [DOI 10.1148/radiol.2017162100; PMID 28301777](https://pubmed.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/28301777/): jointly detected/localized Genant-type vertebral compression and calculated vertebral bone density, directly establishing that both proposed quantities can be extracted together. (ii) Nicolaes et al., “Detection of vertebral fractures in CT using 3D Convolutional Neural Networks,” [arXiv:1911.01816](https://arxiv.org/abs/1911.01816): learned 3D CT features for patient- and vertebra-level compression-fracture detection with explicit localization. (iii) Inigo et al., “An Intrinsically Explainable Approach to Detecting Vertebral Compression Fractures in CT Scans via Neurosymbolic Modeling,” [arXiv:2412.17258](https://arxiv.org/abs/2412.17258): combined learned vertebral segmentation with rule-based height-distribution analysis for interpretable CT fracture detection.
2. **Delta.** The candidate does not invent density-versus-morphometry measurement; it uniquely decomposes a frozen generalist model's osteoporosis score between those two established quantities and adds a preregistered vertebral-level concentration test.
3. **Why not done — `NEW_CAPABILITY`.** Merlin ([arXiv:2406.06512](https://arxiv.org/abs/2406.06512)) newly provides an open abdominal foundation model with an osteoporosis output whose latent cue choice can be audited; prior systems were purpose-built osteoporosis or fracture detectors.
4. **Verdict — `NOVEL_UNVERIFIED`.** No searched neighbor decomposed Merlin's osteoporosis output, but the underlying joint density/morphometry analysis is mature and the claimed thoracolumbar mechanical interpretation was not independently verified in this audit.

## An airway and its artery run together and should taper together - ask whether the model reads bronchiectasis as the broken ratio between the two — baseline

1. **Neighbors.** (i) Diaz et al., “Artificial Intelligence–based CT Assessment of Bronchiectasis: The COPDGene Study,” *Radiology* 2023, [DOI 10.1148/radiol.221109](https://pmc.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/articles/PMC10068886/): reconstructed airway and vessel trees, separated arteries from veins, paired airways with arteries, and related automated airway-to-artery ratios to visual severity and outcomes. (ii) Beeravolu et al., “An Automated Broncho-Arterial (BA) Pair Segmentation Process and Assessment of BA Ratios in Children with Bronchiectasis Using Lung HRCT Scans: A Pilot Study,” *Diagnostics* 2023, [DOI 10.3390/diagnostics13142361; PMID 37509513](https://pmc.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/articles/PMC10376950/): automatically detected BA pairs and calculated multiple diameter ratios on HRCT. (iii) Chen et al., “Automatic analysis of bronchus-artery ratios and mucus plugs of 640 chest CTs of EMBARC bronchiectasis patients,” *European Respiratory Journal* 2024 conference abstract PA652, [DOI 10.1183/13993003.congress-2024.PA652](https://publications.ersnet.org/content/erj/64/suppl68/pa652): applied automated bronchus–artery and mucus-plug quantification at registry scale.
2. **Delta.** Automated BAR is not new; the candidate's precise delta is to compare BAR with absolute airway calibre as explanations of CT-CLIP's frozen bronchiectasis score.
3. **Why not done — `NEW_CAPABILITY`.** CT-CLIP ([arXiv:2403.17834](https://arxiv.org/abs/2403.17834)) newly supplies a released generalist bronchiectasis output to audit, whereas the closest BAR studies quantified disease directly and did not explain a foundation model.
4. **Verdict — `NOVEL_UNVERIFIED`.** No searched work linked CT-CLIP's score to BAR, but the measurement and absolute-versus-relative-calibre debate are established; novelty is confined to the model-specific audit, and incomplete indexing of recent foundation-model probes prevents a verified verdict.
