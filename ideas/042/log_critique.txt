The three findings that matter most:

1. **The dispersion edit is internally contradictory as specified.** The card demands a contraction that preserves arrival time, area, *and* peak height while narrowing the curve — but at fixed area and peak, reducing variance forces boxcar-like shapes that its own realism gate (interpolation between observed same-case curves) must reject, because real curves couple width and peak through area conservation. Letting the peak rise (the physical behavior) reintroduces a trivial intensity confound the delay arm doesn't control. I proposed three repairs (two-arm concordance, an explicit peak-only control, or an honest "curve-shape bundle" restatement of the estimand); the question itself survives.

2. **The card missed prior work its own program had already found.** Scout cycle 003's candidate c06 ("The bolus spreads like dye in a river") is essentially the same intervention, and its novelty audit named Amador et al., ISBI 2024 (attention analysis of a raw-4D-CTP outcome model) as the closest prior work with a mandatory full read before advancement.
