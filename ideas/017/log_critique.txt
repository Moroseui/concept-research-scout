Critique written to `ideas/017/critique.md`. **Decision: ADVANCE TO REVISION** — the question survives, but the confirmatory experiment as designed cannot answer it.

**The decisive objection:** the card's "selective use" test is linear concept-direction erasure in frozen Sybil embeddings, compared against emphysema/sex/lung-volume erasures. That method is documented to remove correlated non-concept features even under favorable conditions ([Kumar, Tan & Sharma, NeurIPS 2022](https://arxiv.org/abs/2207.04153); collateral-damage concerns in amnesic probing; [LEACE](https://arxiv.org/abs/2306.03819) guarantees only linear guarding). And this idea is the worst case for it: the founding literature says saber-sheath trachea occurs almost exclusively in men with COPD ([Greene 1978, AJR](https://ajronline.org/doi/10.2214/ajr.130.3.441): the defining series is 60 male patients, 95% with clinical COPD), so the concept directions the design must separate are constitutively entangled. Neither a positive nor a null erasure result identifies tracheal-shape use — which also invalidates the card's "decisive negative" classification and its identifiability score of 4 (I recompute Mode C priority at ~4.1, down from 4.6, still strong).

**Other findings from verification:**
- [Pompe et al.](https://pmc.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/articles/PMC6052793/) checks out as the X-measurement source (fully automated, COPDGene n=200), but saber-sheath prevalence was only 5.5% even in that COPD-enriched cohort — the keystone's "enough low-index cases independent of sex and emphysema" is probably false categorically; the study must run on the continuous index. The card's `dies_like_prior` is aimed at the wrong prior: the real threat is idea-009's IDENTIFIABILITY_FAILURE, and I made the Stage 0 partial-correlation audit the explicit test of that.
- The respiratory-stability gate can't run as written: NLST has no expiratory scans. I propose replacing it with NLST's T0/T1/T2 annual repeats as a within-subject trait-stability test — a cleaner probe of "fixed remodeling" that needs no new data.
- Two points in the idea's favor that the card didn't know: NLST images plus limited clinical data are now publicly downloadable via TCIA/IDC ([CDAS project 3028](https://cdas.cancer.gov/approved-projects/3028)) with no DUA, and my bounded search found no prior work linking tracheal index to any lung-cancer model — the closest is the nodule-focused ["Auditing Sybil"](https://arxiv.org/html/2602.02560v2) paper, which never measures the trachea.
- One gap the card missed entirely: Sybil was trained on NLST, so associations computed on training-set scans are contaminated — the held-out-split recovery problem idea-017 silently inherits from idea-008.

**Constructive close:** the nearest defensible version — a one-week Stage 0 on public NLST testing preprocessing survival, trait stability across annual repeats, and confound-adjusted association — keeps the same deliverable sentence, so under the claim-identity rule it's revision-in-place, not a new candidate. It's worth doing: every one of its three outcomes is informative, and the collinearity answer also prices the other pending Sybil-anatomy candidates (scout-008-c05, scout-009-c05).
