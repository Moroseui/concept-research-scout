## Duty 1 — Connection map

**idea-004 ↔ idea-013 ↔ scout-006-c05 ↔ scout-007-c07.** Idea-004's 425 geometry-matched same-acquisition reconstruction pairs are not merely a CT-CLIP benchmark audit. They are a ready-made physical repeatability floor for three proposed X measurements on CT-RATE: calcium burden in idea-013, bronchoarterial ratio in scout-006-c05, and subpleural cyst topology in scout-007-c07. If an X changes materially between reconstructions of identical anatomy, a score–X relation cannot identify anatomy without reconstruction stratification. Conversely, stable X but unstable CT-CLIP output implicates the model/pipeline rather than the quantifier. The pairs do not validate any X biologically or prove model use.

**idea-004 ↔ idea-006.** The endorsed exterior-swap spin-off from idea-006 is structurally an extension of idea-004: use geometry-matched reconstructions of the same acquisition, preserve every voxel inside a dilated body contour, and exchange only the exterior. Idea-004 supplies linkage, pair counts, and contrast strata; the swap supplies the positive exterior-reliance estimand that the original patient-deletion intervention lacked. This is a connection, not a revival: the dossier already records the spin-off, and no new fact establishes edit validity or licenses reincorporating it into idea-006.

**idea-008 ↔ idea-009 ↔ idea-012.** These are three proposed explanations for the same published class of non-nodular Sybil risk signal: emphysema, pulmonary vascular pruning, and coronary calcium. Their observational arms are therefore mutually constraining rather than independent discoveries. A positive single-feature association is expected under shared smoking, age, and cardiopulmonary disease; a credible common cohort would have to measure all three and treat them as rivals. Idea-012's unavailable scan-level residual-cohort membership blocks the exact published-residual claim for the whole trio, while idea-009's rejected estimand shows that joint regression would still establish association, not natural feature use.

**idea-007 ↔ scout-006-c05 ↔ scout-007-c07.** Inspiration is a shared hidden variable. Lung volume changes parenchymal attenuation and airway/artery calibre, and it can change the apparent separation of small subpleural cyst walls after resampling. Thus idea-007's paired inhale/exhale corpus can serve as a nuisance-sensitivity audit for the bronchoarterial-ratio and honeycomb-topology extractors before either is interpreted as disease morphology. It cannot establish bronchiectasis or fibrosis use, because respiratory state changes several anatomical and density quantities simultaneously.

**scout-006-c05 ↔ scout-007-c07.** Bronchoarterial-ratio dilation and honeycomb topology meet at traction bronchiectasis/bronchiolectasis. A fibrosis score that follows “holes at the pleural edge” may instead be following dilated peripheral airways; the honeycomb extractor's airway-tree exclusion and the airway/artery pairing pipeline should therefore be evaluated together. A 2025 external-validation study reports that an automated interstitial-lung-abnormality score rises with reader-graded traction bronchiectasis severity, which strengthens this alternative explanation but does not test either proposed model-use mechanism ([PMID 41372552](https://pubmed.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/41372552/)).
