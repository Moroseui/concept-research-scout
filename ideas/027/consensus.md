# Debate summary — idea 027

## Agreed

- In round 1, both sides agreed that the original edit was cross-modally incoherent: it installed the NCCT/CTA appearance of isolated sulcal effacement (ISE) while freezing perfusion, although the cited clinical phenotype included prolonged Tmax and normal-to-increased CBV. A response to that edit could therefore reflect impossible modality combinations or synthesis artifacts rather than use of collateral-supported viability.
- In rounds 1–2, both sides accepted a repair at the phenotype-category level: restrict recipients to territories already having ISE-congruent perfusion, require blinded-reader and modality-consistency realism gates, and compare the coherent edit against matched joint-distribution-preserving shams. Both sides also accepted that this only supports rung 1 because the small clinical series does not establish the phenotype's fine conditional structure.
- In rounds 1–2, both sides agreed that the original annotation-free claim was untenable. Blinded expert review of both apparent positive and negative territories is needed to validate the automatic construct and the edit-realism gate, creating fresh annotation burden.
- In round 2, both sides agreed that no located, independently existing checkpoint both performs non-trivially and is verified to receive the NCCT and CTA intensities carrying the sign. On the available account, the published winner may use NCCT only to create a brain mask, while the public raw-CTP alternative neither receives the proposed triad nor performs adequately.
- In rounds 2–3, both sides agreed that a newly trained full-trilogy model could be studied with a frozen recipe, executable-path channel verification, pair-excluded scoring, disjoint development data, and performance kill gates. They also agreed that this would test behavior learned by an investigator-designed model family, not audit behavior of the independently existing benchmark model contemplated at round zero.
- In round 3, both sides agreed that this change of audited object changes the deliverable sentence and therefore triggers the repository's claim-identity rule: the bespoke-model study must be a successor with idea 027 as parent, not a revision in place.
- In rounds 1–3, both sides agreed that support is likely marginal. The original estimate was about 11 positives among 149 cases; perfusion-congruent pairing and a development quarantine would reduce expected usable support further, to roughly nine donors before pair restrictions. Any successor needs a precision-derived census gate rather than the original arbitrary threshold of 15 territories.

## Unresolved

### Does a suitable independently existing checkpoint exist?

- **Question:** Is there an independently existing, frozen, non-trivially performing final-infarct checkpoint whose original forward path consumes both NCCT and CTA voxel intensities?
- **Proposer's position:** No such asset was located; the published winner appears blind to NCCT intensities and the public raw-CTP alternative is unsuitable. The proposer would not assume another checkpoint exists without verification.
- **Critic's position:** Such a checkpoint is the only route to preserving idea 027 as an audit of existing model behavior.
- **What evidence would settle it:** A checkpoint artifact, held-out performance evidence, and inspection of its executable preprocessing and forward path showing that registered NCCT and CTA voxel intensities—not only an NCCT-derived mask—reach the network. No such evidence was presented.

### Is there enough support for a successor study?

- **Question:** After construct validation, perfusion-congruence filtering, location matching, development quarantine, and patient-level independence, are there enough eligible ISE donor–recipient pairs for a precise result?
- **Proposer's position:** Probably not; the expected support falls to about nine donors before pair restrictions, so a Stage-0 census is expected to kill the bespoke-model successor.
- **Critic's position:** The original `>=15` threshold is not a power calculation, and no confirmatory study is justified without an effect- or precision-based support requirement.
- **What evidence would settle it:** A blinded, outcome-free census using an expert-validated construct, followed by a prespecified patient-clustered power or confidence-interval calculation over eligible pairs. This would settle feasibility only, not model-use identifiability.

### Can edited examples preserve the relevant joint distribution closely enough?

- **Question:** Does perfusion-congruent recipient selection plus realism and sham gates make cross-patient triad transplantation adequate for a rung-1 use claim?
- **Proposer's position:** It repairs the categorical cross-modal incoherence, while acknowledging that donor–recipient texture and geometry mismatch remains and that only rung 1 is supportable.
- **Critic's position:** The round-1 amendment adequately repairs the original phenotype-category objection, conditional on the proposed held-out realism and sham gates.
- **What evidence would settle it:** Prespecified held-out evidence that blinded experts and modality-consistency tests cannot distinguish edits from real ISE territories, together with response exceedance over matched shams. Fine-grained physiological coherence cannot be established from the cited eight-case series alone and would require richer external data.

There is no remaining disagreement about the disposition of idea 027 under its original deliverable. The unresolved items concern whether future evidence could reopen it or support successors.

## Positions that moved

- **Proposer, round 1:** Conceded that freezing perfusion while installing the NCCT/CTA triad invalidated the original intervention. This followed the critic's source-backed argument that ISE is a joint phenotype including prolonged Tmax and preserved or elevated CBV. The proposer amended the design to perfusion-congruent recipient selection, blinded realism testing, and sham exceedance. This was an earned concession.
- **Critic, round 2:** Accepted that the round-1 amendment repaired the categorical cross-modal-coherence objection and preserved the question at that point. This acceptance followed concrete new gates rather than capitulation.
- **Proposer, round 2:** Conceded the absence of a verified suitable checkpoint and replaced the audit asset with a newly trained, pair-excluded full-trilogy ensemble. This directly answered the critic's model-asset and leakage argument, but introduced a claim-identity problem.
- **Critic, round 3:** Accepted that the proposed training, channel-receipt, exclusion, and performance gates could make the bespoke model technically studyable, while arguing that they answer a successor question. This was a narrowing of the objection, not a concession on disposition.
- **Proposer, round 3:** Conceded that the bespoke model changes both the audited object and deliverable sentence, after the critic applied the repository's 2026-08-10 claim-identity rule and distinguished auditing existing behavior from creating an architecture capable of learning the sign. The proposer had expressly exposed this identity fork in round 2, and the critic supplied the decisive governance argument; the concession was earned, not UNEARNED.

## Amendments made

At round zero, idea 027 claimed that an existing final-infarct model uses isolated sulcal effacement with preserved gray-white differentiation as a tissue-survival signal. It proposed swapping NCCT/CTA triad features while holding perfusion fixed, required no expert annotation, assumed an unspecified model, estimated under 15 GPU-hours after training, and used a `>=15`-territory support gate.

Round 1 restricted edits to recipients already showing prolonged Tmax and preserved or elevated CBV, added blinded-reader and modality-consistency realism gates, and required response beyond matched joint-distribution-preserving shams. It lost the annotation-free claim, reduced support, and capped interpretation at rung 1.

Round 2 proposed a new full-trilogy residual nnU-Net family with verified NCCT/CTA channel receipt, a roughly 30-patient development quarantine, five-fold pair exclusion requiring ten trainings, and per-model held-out performance kill gates. It lost the benchmark-audit framing, increased compute to roughly 100+ GPU-hours and several weeks, and further reduced expected donor support.

Round 3 did not adopt that bespoke-model amendment into idea 027. Both sides classified it as a successor because its honest deliverable is learnability/use by an investigator-trained full-trilogy model, not use by the existing final-infarct model. The observational ISE replication and code-level verification of the winner's input channels were also recorded as separate possible successors, not repairs to this card.

## Recommendation

REJECT idea 027 as superseded. The original intervention was invalid, and the only fully specified model path changes the audited object and deliverable sentence. Under the repository's claim-identity rule, that technically defensible path must be registered as a successor rather than used to keep this candidate alive.

The single most important thing for the human to inspect is whether any independently existing, performant final-infarct checkpoint can be verified from its executable path to consume NCCT and CTA intensities. If none exists, the original audit cannot be reopened; the bespoke learnability study, observational ISE replication, and benchmark-input audit should be considered separately on their own merits.

## In plain terms

This idea asks whether an existing stroke model treats a rare pattern of flattened brain grooves, preserved tissue appearance, and enlarged rescue vessels as evidence that tissue will survive. It originally tried to test that by editing scans.

The debate concluded that the original edit created an unnatural mismatch between scan types, and that no suitable existing model was found that could even see all parts of the pattern. Training a new model could support a study, but it would ask whether a purpose-built model can learn the signal, which is a different question and must be registered separately. The human is being asked to check whether a suitable pre-existing model can be verified before accepting that the original audit is closed.
