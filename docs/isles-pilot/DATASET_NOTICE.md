# ISLES24 dataset attribution and publication boundary

Verified 2026-09-05 against the [immutable release record](https://zenodo.org/records/16813698)
and its [API metadata](https://zenodo.org/api/records/16813698):
**ISLES'24 — A Real-World Longitudinal Multimodal Stroke Dataset**, Riedel,
de la Rosa and colleagues, DOI **10.5281/zenodo.16813698**, released 2025-08-12.
License: [Creative Commons Attribution–NonCommercial–ShareAlike 4.0](https://creativecommons.org/licenses/by-nc-sa/4.0/).
Reuse must retain attribution, link the license, indicate modifications and
observe noncommercial/share-alike terms where applicable. No endorsement implied.
These terms concern the dataset and applicable adaptations, not an automatic
replacement of the repository's overall software license.

The record requests citation of the dataset paper and the challenge paper:
Riedel et al., *ISLES'24 — A Real-World Longitudinal Multimodal Stroke Dataset*;
de la Rosa et al., *ISLES'24: Final Infarct Prediction with Multimodal Imaging
and Clinical Data. Where Do We Stand?*, arXiv:2408.10966. Its description gives
2408.09259 for the dataset paper whereas its structured related identifier gives
2408.11142; that inconsistency is recorded rather than silently harmonized.
Circle of Willis use additionally requires the cited TopCoW work; this pilot
baseline does not use those masks.

The record lists only train.7z and clinical_data-description.xlsx. The already
frozen archive member manifest contains no path matching license/readme/notice
(case-insensitive inspection). No archive was acquired to search for other
notices. Existing dictionary is locally available. Accompanying notice coverage
is therefore limited to the record and available manifest, not an assertion
that no other notice can exist.

047 copied staged raw phenotype CSVs across its aggregate-only publication
boundary. That is a confirmed project violation regardless of the reuse license.
The license does not categorically prohibit redistribution. A distinct licensing
breach has not been established by this audit; attribution was scattered and
this notice makes the dataset terms and citation requirement explicit. No
commercial use or share-alike incompatibility is inferred without evidence.
Original source evidence is preserved privately for the cleanup review.

The [dataset paper itself](https://arxiv.org/abs/2408.11142) confirms the
structured record identifier 2408.11142 and the requested title; use that paper
in bibliographies while retaining the metadata-discrepancy note above.
