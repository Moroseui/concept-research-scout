Item 6's primary gain is +0.04043807 [0.02796645,0.05423991]; its common-support sensitivity is the matching Colab primary above. 2. Bootstrap ordering: both use 2,000 draws and seed 0, but item 6 accumulates patients by fold and then patient, while Colab uses globally sorted patient order. Identical RNG indices therefore select different patients. Additionally, item 6 drops undefined values before sampling, whereas Colab samples all patients and uses nanmean. These source differences account for nonidentical Monte Carlo interval procedures despite matching means. No patient-level bootstrap rerun was performed. Do not call the near-zero lesion-F1 interval a reproduced positive finding: item 6 common-support interval includes a small negative value, and Colab's displayed lower bound rounds to +0.000.
