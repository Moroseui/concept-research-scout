## Uncertainty, inconsistencies and overlap

Voxel F1 is `2TP/(2TP+FP+FN)` and equals binary Dice when mask domains and empty-mask rules agree. Lesion-wise F1 counts matched, missed and false-positive connected components under the particular matching procedure. Sprint4/5 any-overlap detection is a separate diagnostic, not automatically the later lesion-F1 implementation. Full truth must remain in overlap/recall/volume calculations; feature-mask ranking metrics use a different domain. An undefined ranking score when only one truth class is present is not zero performance. [Sprint4 C6/source `component_detection`; Sprint5b closeout §3; Sprint8/9 result denominators; S9-04.1.]

The Sprint3b summary confused the median of patientwise error changes with the difference of group medians. Its original review preserves the resulting incorrect classification of the territory-cutoff result; Sprint4's `paired_stats` computes the intended difference of medians and retains patientwise change separately. Later tables explicitly use median patientwise change, a legitimate different estimand. In particular, subtracting Sprint10's displayed absolute-error medians does not reproduce its paired-error contrast. Signed bias, absolute error, mean patient Dice and pooled voxel Dice are not interchangeable. [doc-0b520a638a51964f-view.txt §5; Sprint4 C6/source; Sprint10 C9/O2.]

The strongest recurring development observation is placement improvement from smoothing. It appears in sequential and repeated-fold evidence, but those analyses overlap. Sprint3/3b repeat recipes; Sprint4/5/5b reuse the same repeatedly inspected comparison group; Sprint10 consumes Sprint8/9 outputs; Sprint11 reproduces Sprint8 seed-1 rows and overlaps Sprint12's reference/admission/expanded rows; Sprint12b deliberately reproduces earlier normalization/reference tables. None is an independent new-patient replication. Source-image comparisons change grids and support, so the series is not a single controlled performance ladder.

Small-lesion failure remains visible across pipelines.
