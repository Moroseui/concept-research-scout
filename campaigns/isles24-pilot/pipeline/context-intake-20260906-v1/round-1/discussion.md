## Keep four concepts separate

**Scientific context** describes a landscape: questions, assumptions, available sources, uncertainties and relationships. It can contain competing explanations and unresolved disagreements. It does not prescribe a target, metric, experiment order or acceptable answer.

**Charter** records chosen objectives, scope and evaluation criteria. The supplied human-authored `charters/isles24/CHARTER.md` and the preview `isles24-prediction/proposed-v1` have distinct authority and status. A descriptive context may relate to both without adopting the latter. Informal discussion needs no new charter. Existing charter-specific scores remain untouched and no scores are computed here.

**Scientific blocker** is an unresolved question or assumption that obstructs a specified inference. It needs a source, an honest last-check record and an observable criterion for progress. “Blocker” applies to that inference, not automatically to every related task. Some uncertainties remain reportable limitations rather than conditions that stop P001.

**Operational dependency** concerns access, authentication, runtime, resources, evidence custody or permission. Unavailable execution does not make an idea scientifically worthless. A missing original console is an evidence-provenance dependency; scientific landing is an authority/lifecycle dependency. Neither becomes a scientific hypothesis simply because it prevents an interpretation from being used.
